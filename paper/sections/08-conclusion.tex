\section{Conclusion}

MIDI-Instruct defines instruction-conditioned symbolic music editing as a benchmarkable task with dual edit and preserve metrics, a separate MIDI-Reason plan track, and an open synthetic dataset generator. By grading emitted MIDI files with programmatic predicates rather than LLM judges, the benchmark supports reproducible comparison of future text-to-MIDI systems, editing models, and tool-augmented agents on a shared schema. Constraint-gold items and licensed seed corpora are planned once the unique-gold grader is validated at scale.
