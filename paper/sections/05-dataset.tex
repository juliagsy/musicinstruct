\section{Dataset construction}

\subsection{Seeds}

Version~0.1 uses 12 programmatic multi-track seeds (piano, bass, drums; four bars each) generated by the toolkit and distributed under the repository license. Each seed defines a \texttt{composition\_id}; all derived items inherit the same split assignment to prevent leakage.

\subsection{Splits}

Seeds 0--7 $\to$ train, 8--9 $\to$ validation, 10--11 $\to$ test. Future releases may add licensed corpora (Mutopia, Lakh subsets) with the same split policy.

\subsection{Scale}

The generator emits items from the Cartesian product of seeds, operator parameters, and instruction templates. Full generation yields 336 items: 192 transpose (8 semitone steps $\times$ 2 phrasings), 36 velocity, 36 tempo, 24 mute, and 48 program change. The released pilot trims proportionally within each composition so every seed remains represented. Its 300 items comprise 168 transpose, 24 velocity, 36 tempo, 24 mute, and 48 program-change examples, split into 200 train, 50 validation, and 50 test items.

\subsection{Reproducibility}

\begin{verbatim}
musicinstruct generate-pilot --output-dir data/pilot --target 300
musicinstruct validate data/pilot/pilot.jsonl
musicinstruct self-test data/pilot/pilot.jsonl
\end{verbatim}

Self-test scores gold MIDI against itself; joint pass rate should be 1.0, validating the grader before model experiments.
